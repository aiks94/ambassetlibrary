TO PUT IT ONLINE
1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in.
2. Drag this .zip file onto the page.
3. Done. Netlify gives you a web address you can share.

TO UPDATE IT LATER
In the claude.ai library, open Settings > Backup > Download website for Netlify (.zip),
then in Netlify open your site > Deploys and drag the new .zip onto the page.

GOOD TO KNOW
- This version is read-only: browse, search, preview, download, copy filenames, use the naming generator and download colours.
- It includes approved, non-archived assets only. Uploads, approvals and editing stay in the claude.ai library.
- Anyone with the link can open it. Search engines are asked not to list it.
